C. Build materialized views on top of the sales table to aggregate data at the day and month level.

Materialized views are precomputed views that cache the results of a query, which can significantly improve query performance and reduce costs by avoiding repeated computation. They automatically update with changes to the base table, ensuring data freshness without additional maintenance.
