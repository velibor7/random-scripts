Auto-discovery needs to be enabled for the curated zone to ensure that Dataplex can scan and register the files. You can configure this setting at the zone or asset level.
Option A, moving the JSON and CSV files to the raw zone, would not solve the issue of automatic discovery in the curated zone. The problem lies in the configuration of the curated zone, not the location of the files.
