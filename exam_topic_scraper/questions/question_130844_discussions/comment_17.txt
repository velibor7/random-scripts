A common reason for underutilized TPUs is a small batch size. TPUs are designed for high throughput, and feeding them small batches doesn't leverage their full potential.
Try increasing the batch size while monitoring model performance. A larger batch size can lead to faster training but might also affect accuracy. Experiment to find the optimal balance.
